    • Defect Vision New

      An industrial visual-inspection pipeline that classifies surface defects, flags unseen anomalies and draws where the defect is — then shrinks the model for edge devices.

      PythonPyTorchOpenCVONNXFastAPI
      99.75%
      validation accuracy, 0.996 macro-F1
      Code
      • CNN trained on a 4,000-image, imbalanced synthetic dataset of scratches, spots and cracks.
      • Autoencoder trained only on good parts catches 90.4% of defects at a 1% false-alarm rate.
      • OpenCV CLAHE preprocessing, augmentation and class-balanced sampling.
      • Localises defects with residual thresholding, morphology and contours into boxes and masks.
      • int8 quantization shrinks the model from 354 KB to 123 KB at equal accuracy; ONNX export with a parity test.
      • FastAPI inspection endpoint returns a pass/reject verdict per part image.
